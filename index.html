View Ceremony songs →
  1. 02

    Groomsmen walk down the aisle

    Live bagpipes Matthew (bagpiper) We’ve hired Matthew as our bagpiper. He will play the groomsmen down the aisle.
  2. 03

    Bridesmaids walk down the aisle

    We’ve Only Just Begun The Carpenters This is the song the bridesmaids will walk down the aisle to.
  3. 04

    Bride walks down the mountain aisle

    Start at approx. 0:42 Archie, Marry Me Flyte The bride will walk down the aisle to the Flyte version. Start when he sings “Hey, hey, marry me Archie.”
  4. 05

    Ceremony ends and everyone cheers

    Start at approx. 2:13 Lover Taylor Swift As the ceremony ends and everyone cheers, please play “Lover” beginning with “Ladies and gentlemen, will you please stand?” Let the song play to the very end. Once the song is finished, Matthew will begin playing the bagpipes.
  5. 06

    Orchard petal and confetti toss

    Live bagpipes Matthew (bagpiper) Once “Lover” finishes, Matthew will begin playing bagpipes and lead everyone up the hill to the reception. When everyone reaches the orchard, the petal and confetti toss will happen while Matthew continues playing.
